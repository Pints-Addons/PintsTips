Pint's Tips v1.1 - better tooltips
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Works on its own; with Pint's Alts it also shows what your other characters have.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsTips
  (or _forever_ for WoW Forever). Restart the game.

WHAT IT DOES
  Items
  - How many of the item each of your characters has - in bags, bank, mail or worn - and the total.
    (Your other characters come from Pint's Alts, which remembers them each time you log in on them.)
  - What it's used for: which professions use it and at what skill, which quests want it, which class spells
    need it. For recipes: which of your characters already know it, or have the skill to learn it.
  - Item level and the item's GearScore.
  Players
  - Class-coloured name and title, guild and guild rank, talents, GearScore and average item level, and who
    they're targeting.
  - Hold Shift over a player to see their talent trees beside the tooltip.
  Character window: your GearScore and item level.
  Tooltip position: Blizzard's corner, at the mouse, or a spot you choose.
  Other add-ons that read TacoTip's GearScore keep working without TacoTip.

HOW TO USE
  /tips            options (every part can be switched on or off)
  /tips minimap    show or hide the minimap button
