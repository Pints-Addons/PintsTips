Pint's Tips - Changelog

v1.2.2
- Players' tooltips show their zone - your group members' wherever they are, anyone else's (they're near you) as
  your own spot and zone. "Players: their zone" in /tips switches it off.

v1.2.1
- WoW Forever: talent trees (Shift over a player) were broken for some classes - a hunter's showed only 3 talents, a
  priest's tiny jumbled icons in a very tall window. A few unused talents sit far outside the trees in the game's data
  and stretched the layout; they're left out now.

v1.2
- Your history in tooltips: how many of a creature you've killed (this character), and your duel record and time
  grouped with other players.
- Notes and ratings: write a note about a player, creature, item or quest - /tips note (your target or what's under
  the mouse; or /tips note [item link], /tips note quest, /tips note <a title>). Rate a player's behaviour and skill
  (-- to ++). A preview and the rating show in their tooltips; /tips notes lists them all, with a search. Notes are
  shared by all your characters.
- WoW Forever: "Added in WoW Forever" on items and creatures new there.
- Each can be switched off in /tips.
- Comparing gear: the tooltip of what you're wearing has a bold gold "Currently Equipped" over it.
- Shift: talent trees no longer shows in combat. Switch it off altogether with "Shift: talent trees" in /tips.

v1.1
- Hold Shift over a player to see their talent trees beside the tooltip (option: "Shift: talent trees").
- WoW Forever: fixed a "secret boolean" error in player tooltips during fights.

v1.0
- First public release.
- Item tooltips: how many each of your characters has (bags, bank, mail, worn) with a total - with Pint's Alts.
- What an item is used for: professions (with the skill range), quests and class spells; for recipes, which of
  your characters already know it or could learn it.
- Item level and GearScore on items; GearScore and average item level on players and in the character window.
- Player tooltips: class-coloured name, title, guild and rank, talents and what they're targeting.
- Tooltip position: Blizzard's corner, at the cursor, or a spot you choose.
- Provides TT_GS for add-ons that read TacoTip's GearScore.
- Works on Classic Era and WoW Forever.
